jcapps.dev — Self-hosted tools, no database required

I work at a German industrial supplier. Since late 2025 I've been building internal tools with Claude Code. Two of them are in daily production use; the others are in active internal testing. jcapps.dev is where they land when they're ready for others.

Transfer

v1.3.1 Live

Self-hosted file sharing. Upload a file, get a link, the recipient downloads without an account. Supports large files via chunked upload. It started because we didn't want someone else's branding on links going to customers.
